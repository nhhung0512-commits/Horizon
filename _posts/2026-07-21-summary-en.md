## [Cloudflare Launches Internal DNS Service](https://blog.cloudflare.com/internal-dns/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Cloudflare announced the general availability of its Internal DNS service on July 20, 2026, providing authoritative and recursive DNS for private networks with Zero Trust integration and unified management. This release eliminates the need for separate DNS systems for public and private networks, simplifying split-horizon configurations and extending Zero Trust policies to the DNS layer, which is a major step for enterprise network security and management. The service is included at no extra cost for existing Cloudflare Gateway customers, supports DNS views to manage split-horizon DNS, and can be deployed via API, Terraform, or Cloudflare's WAN.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 21, 03:49

**Background**: Split-horizon DNS (also known as split-view DNS) allows a DNS server to provide different responses based on the source of the query, typically to separate internal and external DNS records. Managing separate DNS servers for public and private networks often leads to data drift and complexity. Cloudflare's Internal DNS integrates both into a single control plane, leveraging its global network and Zero Trust architecture.
